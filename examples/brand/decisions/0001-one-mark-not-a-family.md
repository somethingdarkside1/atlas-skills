# One mark, not a family

The partners wanted a logo family (mark, monogram, pattern) and a launch in six weeks. One wordmark done well covers every surface in the brief, and a family would spend the six weeks on variants nobody asked for. We ship one mark.

Considered: a full family (rejected for time); a symbol only (rejected because the name is unknown).
Revisit when: an app icon or a social avatar is needed, where a wordmark fails.
