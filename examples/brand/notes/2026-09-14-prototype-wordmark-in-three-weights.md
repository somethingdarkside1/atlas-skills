# Wordmark in three weights

## Summary
Three letterform directions were drawn and checked at three sizes. The condensed grotesque is the only one that stays legible at 16 pixels, so it is chosen.

## Detail
Directions: a condensed grotesque, a wide geometric, and a serif with a drawn ligature. At 16 pixels the geometric's counters close and the serif's ligature becomes a smudge. The grotesque holds because its counters are tall.
