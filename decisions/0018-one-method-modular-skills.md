# Atlas is one project method implemented through modular skills

Present Atlas as an integrated method with a full-package default, shared work records, and clear routes from uncertainty to accepted output. Keep operations modular and methods conditionally loaded, with selective installation advertised only where its required context and dependencies pass installation tests; integration of the product does not imply unattended orchestration or a mandatory full workflow for small work. Adopted through the user's instruction to proceed in [core-model/01](../plan/core-model/01-settle-boundaries.md), this makes continuity the product promise while preserving the packaging boundary in [0014](0014-shared-methods-travel-with-consumers.md).

Considered: marketing unrelated independent skills; requiring every operation for every task; full-package-only support regardless of evidence.
Revisit when: adopter evidence favors a different entry point or a standalone method has a demonstrated independent use.
