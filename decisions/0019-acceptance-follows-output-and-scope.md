# Acceptance follows the exact output and applicable scope

Acceptance binds a retrievable output to the applicable brief and task criteria: Git must account for included working changes, and a non-Git delivery needs a preserved version or snapshot with content identity, rather than a date or mutable filename alone. Changed content or scope requires an impact assessment and renewal of affected acceptance while retaining prior evidence; canceled blockers require explicit dependency repair, and dependent work needs the exact accepted output in a project-permitted workspace even before integration. Adopted through the user's instruction to proceed in [core-model/01](../plan/core-model/01-settle-boundaries.md), these principles keep completion trustworthy across media; task 02 owns their fields, transition guards, and recovery representation.

Considered: date-based freshness; closed issues as completion; invalidating all work on every edit; making integration a universal prerequisite.
Revisit when: a supported medium cannot retain usable output identity or an impact assessment repeatedly misses affected acceptance.
