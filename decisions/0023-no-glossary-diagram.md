# The glossary is a list, without a diagram

`/interview-me` and `/map-it` stop drawing a glossary diagram, and the glossary template carries none. The rule "an edge to each term its definition names" cannot be checked for terms of several words, and the redraw cost a step at the end of every interview. The map diagram stays, under [0003](0003-mermaid-in-markdown.md). `/map-it` still reports a term that a definition names and no entry owns. Accepted by Vitali on 2026-09-17 in the work-skills interview, answering choice 6 of the [whole-set review](../notes/2026-09-17-review-skills-as-a-whole.md).

Considered: drawing it only when asked (one more mode in `/map-it`); keeping it (a rule nobody can verify).
Revisit when: readers of a real glossary ask how its terms relate and the list does not answer.
