# Each interview round is chosen from the answers to the last one

`/interview-me` works like a decision tree. A round asks only the questions whose prerequisites are settled, and the next round is composed after weighing the answers, including partial ones, doubts, and questions asked back; it never lists questions that are waiting. An answer of "I don't understand" re-asks that question in plainer words with a concrete case before anything that hangs off it, and a question asked back is answered with the facts before it becomes a new question. Accepted by Vitali on 2026-09-17 in the work-skills interview, replacing the rule in the draft that a capped round names how many questions wait.
