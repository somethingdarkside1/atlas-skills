# Settle consequential choices for the selected outcome

An interview finishes when the selected outcome and its material choices are settled, with later uncertainty explicitly assigned and discovered facts distinguished from user judgments and delegated choices. Retain one interview operation for project and part scope, with the interview owning the brief and its small settled choices; this supersedes [0005](0005-one-interview-for-both-levels.md) and [0008](0008-the-interview-writes-the-brief.md) only as to exhaustive questioning and brief existence as adoption, so a draft brief can exist while its direction remains sketched. Adopted through the user's instruction to proceed in [core-model/01](../plan/core-model/01-settle-boundaries.md), this permits a small task under an existing brief to proceed without another interview while keeping unanswered consequential choices pending.

Considered: exploring every future branch before work; automatically accepting proposed defaults; requiring a fresh interview for every task.
Revisit when: bounded interviews repeatedly omit a decision needed to meet the selected task's criteria.
