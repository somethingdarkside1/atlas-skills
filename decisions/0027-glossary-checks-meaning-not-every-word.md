# Use the glossary to clarify meaning and keep ordinary wording flexible

Propose a glossary entry when a project-specific distinction affects interpretation or behavior. Treat an alternate word as a finding when it creates a real ambiguity or violates an explicit project requirement, with ordinary words and harmless synonyms remaining available. This would align interview and review with the glossary's current ambiguity-focused purpose; Vitali's answer to Q5 is pending.

Considered: requiring an entry for every new word in a brief or task and rejecting every occurrence of an Avoid synonym.
Revisit when: flexible wording repeatedly hides a material distinction that reviewers fail to catch.
