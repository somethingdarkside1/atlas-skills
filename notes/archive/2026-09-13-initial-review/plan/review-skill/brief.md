# Review skill

## Problem
Reviews lack an exact delivery target, assume GitHub self-approval, and infer part completion from task closure alone. See the [framework review](../../../../2026-09-13-review-atlas-framework.md) for the source evidence.

## Outcome
Review evidence is tied to current artifacts and scope, findings are actionable, and completion follows verified acceptance and integration.
