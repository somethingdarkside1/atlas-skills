# 01: Bind review evidence to the delivered revision

## Delivers
A review procedure and evidence format that identify the exact artifact and brief revisions and give a fresh reviewer access to relevant standards and checks.

Priority and source: P1; F04, F22. See the [framework review](../../../../2026-09-13-review-atlas-framework.md).

## Check by
Review an actual defect, deliver a correction on the same day, and edit the brief or artifact after a clean pass.

## Done when
- [ ] A review identifies its delivery, scope, checks run, limits, and disposition.
- [ ] A changed artifact or brief invalidates earlier approval until reviewed again.
- [ ] Findings include evidence, impact, and an actionable repair using the relevant source.
- [ ] Fresh reviewers can inspect applicable project instructions and neighboring work; inline fallback is disclosed.
- [ ] Human-only checks retain their confirmation and revision context.
