# Interview skill

## Problem
Interviews can default consequential answers, lose small choices between sessions, and revise a brief without identifying affected work. See the [framework review](../../../../2026-09-13-review-atlas-framework.md) for the source evidence.

## Outcome
Proportionate interviews that preserve settled choices, expose assumptions, and carry scope revisions into the existing work.
