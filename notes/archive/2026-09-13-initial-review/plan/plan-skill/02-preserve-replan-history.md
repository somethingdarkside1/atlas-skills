# 02: Preserve history when replanning

## Delivers
A replan procedure that reads task content, retains stable ids and cancellations, and identifies the impact of changed scope or removed work.

Priority and source: P1; F11, F20. See the [framework review](../../../../2026-09-13-review-atlas-framework.md).

## Check by
Remove the highest-numbered todo task with incoming dependencies, revise its brief, and add new work while another task is doing.

## Done when
- [ ] Historical task numbers remain reserved after removal or cancellation.
- [ ] Canceled tasks are distinguishable from successful delivery in both trackers.
- [ ] Incoming blockers across all parts are repaired or surfaced without silently rewriting active or completed work.
- [ ] Human edits and acceptance intent survive full-body task revision.
