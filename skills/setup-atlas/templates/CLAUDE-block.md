## Atlas (atlas: 3)

Atlas keeps project terms in GLOSSARY.md, responsibilities in MAP.md, briefs and tasks in plan/, consequential choices in decisions/, and dated evidence in notes/. Start with the user's target and its map/plan links; expand to relevant terms, decisions, dependencies, and affected parts. Each home owns its format. The working rules in plan/README.md govern task progress, waiting, evidence, and resumption. Use /atlas when the next action is unclear.

Project instructions own saving and external delivery. Atlas follows existing authorization and preserves unrelated work. With no delivery policy, save files in the workspace and report their state.
