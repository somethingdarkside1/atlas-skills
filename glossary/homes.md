# Homes

**Home**:
The authoritative place for one kind of current project knowledge, or the dated record of an observation.
_Avoid_: source of truth

**Glossary**:
The home of project-specific terms and distinctions.
_Avoid_: context

**Map**:
The home of the project shape, with one section per part and a view derived from those sections.
_Avoid_: architecture doc

**Plan**:
The home of briefs and tasks for the active work.
_Avoid_: backlog, tracker

**Decision**:
A recorded consequential choice with its reason and actual adoption state.
_Avoid_: ADR

**Note**:
A dated record of a handoff, experiment, research, or review; durable conclusions are linked to their current home.
_Avoid_: scratch

**Part**:
A named responsibility on the map whose scope and progress can be understood on their own.
_Avoid_: component, area
